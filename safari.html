NATIONAL PARK

Yala Wildlife Safari

Leopard country. Wild encounters.

Explore a landscape of rocky outcrops, dry woodland and open clearings where Sri Lankan leopards make their home. Look out for elephants, spotted deer and colourful birdlife along the way. Every visit unfolds differently; wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

Udawalawe Elephant Safari

Room to roam. Time to explore.

Discover wide grasslands and reservoir scenery in one of Sri Lanka’s best-known elephant habitats. Watch for herds moving through the landscape, with buffalo, peacocks and other birdlife adding to the experience. Animal movements vary with the day and season.

Bundala Birdwatching Safari

Slow down by the lagoons.

Take a closer look at Sri Lanka’s coastal wetlands, where shallow lagoons and salt marshes shelter a rich variety of birds. Painted storks, egrets and other waterbirds bring the scenery to life, while seasonal visitors make each trip a little different.
