How do I rent a bike in Sri Lanka?

Select your preferred bike, choose the rental duration, and complete your booking online or at our rental location.

Do you need a license to drive in Sri Lanka?

A valid motorcycle license from your home country is required to legally ride scooters in Sri Lanka.

Who is responsible for the rented bike?

Once rented, you are responsible for all accident damages, legal matters, and traffic violations during rental.
