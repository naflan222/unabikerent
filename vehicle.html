Before you book

Useful answers for your bike or car rental in Tangalle.

How do I book, and when is my rental confirmed?

Choose a vehicle and send your dates and pickup location to Una Bike Rental on WhatsApp. Our team will confirm availability, the rental quote, and collection or delivery details. Sending a request does not reserve a vehicle until we confirm it with you.

Are helmets included, and is there a kilometre limit?

Helmets are included with bike rentals. Tell us how many riders are travelling so we can arrange them. Our rentals include unlimited kilometres, with no daily distance limit.

Can you deliver to my hotel or arrange another return location?

Delivery is available. Share your hotel or preferred pickup and return locations when requesting a quote. We will confirm whether we can serve those locations, the timing, and any additional charges before you book.

How much does it cost? Are there long-term discounts?

Request a quote for your model and dates. We offer long-term discounts based on the rental period. Before confirming, ask our team about the total rental price, any deposit, payment arrangements, fuel policy, and cancellation terms.

What licence and documents should I bring?

Bring a driving licence valid for the vehicle category in Sri Lanka and your identification. Overseas visitors should check whether a local recognition permit or other authorisation is needed. Ask our team to confirm the documents for your rental before pickup.

What should I check before driving away?

Check and photograph the vehicle condition with our team, and confirm fuel level, return time, insurance cover, any excess, and responsibility for damage. Ask for the contact number to use if you need help during the rental.
